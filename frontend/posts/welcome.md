## Why start a blog?

A lot of the things I know today came from random blog posts written by strangers who simply wanted to share what they discovered.

Someone took the time to write about an error message.

Someone explained why a command didn't work.

Someone documented a project that inspired another.

Those posts helped me more times than I can count.

So I thought,

> Why not do the same?

Maybe one day someone searching the internet at **2 AM** will stumble upon one of my posts and finally solve the problem they've been stuck on.

Even if nobody does, future me definitely will.
